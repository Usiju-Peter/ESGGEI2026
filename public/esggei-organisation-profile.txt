ESGGEI works on community empowerment through education, sustainable agriculture,
literacy, gender advocacy and social support in Nigeria.

Programme priorities described on the website:
- Educational sponsorships, back-to-school campaigns and literacy support.
- Women and youth agricultural training and sustainable livelihoods.
- Gender advocacy and girl child protection.
